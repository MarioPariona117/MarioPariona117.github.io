In an attempt to glorify God, many beautiful prayers have been humbly collected here for my personal use. These are not at all to show off, nor to be gathered merely for the sake of it, but rather to serve as an ordinary means to grow in Love for God and my neighbour.

The understanding that I should intentionally include the Saints in my life, as friends in Heaven, has been with me since 2025. The inspiration to learn about them more deeply then came to me in August 2026, and since then I have been making a personal database to help me do so.
